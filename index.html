#1 CaseTutor — Best Overall

ProsCons
Strong value; clear specialist focusNarrower lineup than the category leader

CaseTutor is an AI-powered interview-practice platform built for consulting candidates, including undergraduate and MBA students, career switchers, experienced professionals and current consultants. Its core experience simulates realistic case interviews through voice, transcription and structured feedback across opening, structuring, analysis and recommendation. The platform also supports firm-specific practice, behavioral interviews, consulting math, a guided Journey roadmap, Story Studio for truthful personal narratives, and Public Speaking Studio for delivery, pacing and presence. CaseTutor positions confidence as a skill built through deliberate practice and aims to democratize access to high-quality consulting interview preparation. The current public site supports practice in English, German, French, Arabic, Hindi and Mandarin.
